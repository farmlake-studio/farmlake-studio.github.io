Privacy Policy for Pocket Potter

Effective Date: November 10, 2026
Summary: Pocket Potter operates entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal information or app data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Pocket Potter is designed as an offline utility application. All data you create, edit, or store within the app remains strictly on your local device. Specifically:

  • We do not collect personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
  • We do not transmit your content, files, or usage activity to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage and Backups

Your app data is stored locally on your device. If you have enabled device backups through Apple's iCloud or your computer, your Pocket Potter data may be included in those encrypted backups in accordance with your Apple ID settings. We do not have access to your iCloud account or backups.

3. Support Communications

If you choose to contact us via email for technical support or feedback, we will receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and improve the app, and we never sell or share it with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Pocket Potter does not collect any personal data from any users, it complies with children's privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added to Pocket Potter. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
