Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Send us an email with your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue. Screenshots are always helpful.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
All subscriptions are managed directly through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name at the top, and select Subscriptions.
